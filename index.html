Beginner's guide to Volleyball


Rules
  • Each team has 6 players on the court at a time.
  • Each team is allowed a maximum of 3 touches before sending the ball over the net.
  • Players must serve the ball from behind the end line to start a rally.
  • A team earns 1 point for every rally it wins. A set is usually played up to 25 points, with a 2-point lead required.
  • Players rotate clockwise when their team wins the right to serve from the opponent.
